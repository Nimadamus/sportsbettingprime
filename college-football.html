College Football

This page is being rebuilt. Its previous daily matchup breakdowns have been removed.

Why this page is empty

Until 21 July 2026 this page published automatically generated college football game breakdowns. An internal audit found that the statistics in those breakdowns — against-the-spread records, points-per-game figures, home records, defensive rankings, line-movement notes and “sharp action” claims — were not sourced from any data provider. They were produced by a random number generator. The schedules shown were also real fixtures from a future month presented as that day’s slate.

We have removed all of it rather than leave it up while we rebuild. The generator that produced it has been switched off. Nothing on this page has been replaced with an estimate, a projection or a placeholder.

Sports Betting Prime does not sell picks and does not publish projected statistics. What returns here will be sourced college football betting fundamentals with the source named on the page.
