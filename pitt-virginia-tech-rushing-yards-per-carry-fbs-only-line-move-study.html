Pitt Allows 2.25 Yards A Carry To FBS Teams, Virginia Tech Allows 3.74, And The Line Moved From -5.5 To -2.5

Pitt and Virginia Tech are both 4 and 0, and both padded that record with an FCS opponent. Pitt beat Bucknell 59 to 0 and Virginia Tech beat VMI 73 to 3. Take those games out and you have three FBS games each, which is a small sample but a real one. Here's what the box scores say, next to what the market has done with the number.

The case against

Every Pitt game has been at home. This is its first road game of the season, at Lane Stadium on a Friday night. Virginia Tech has already won twice away from home. Pitt's offense also managed only 315 yards and 12 points against UCF, so this isn't a team that has blown out every FBS opponent. Three games apiece is a thin sample, and the table above should be read that way.
