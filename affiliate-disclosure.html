Disclosure

Affiliate disclosure

Plainly, in one place: how this site makes money and what that does and does not buy.

We earn commission on sportsbook signups

Sports Betting Prime carries affiliate links to sportsbooks. If you open an account through one of them, we may receive a commission — either a one-off payment or a share of the operator's revenue from your account.

This does not change the odds you get, the bonus you are offered, or anything else about your account. The operator pays us; you do not.

What it does not buy

A commission does not buy a recommendation. We have declined books we would not use ourselves, and where a book has a real drawback we say so on the page rather than omitting it.

It also does not buy placement inside our analysis. Nothing in a guide, a calculator or an article is written to steer you toward a particular operator.

Trust My Record

We link extensively to Trust My Record, which is operated by the same people as this site. That is a related-party relationship and you should read our recommendations of it in that light. We link to it because handicapper records there are timestamped before the game and graded automatically, which is the thing this industry is worst at — but you should verify that claim yourself rather than take ours.

Trust My Record is free to browse and requires no account to view records.

Offer terms change

Bonus amounts, invite codes, rollover requirements and eligible states are set by the operator and change without notice. The terms on the operator's own page always take precedence over anything written here. If you spot something out of date, it is out of date — check theirs.

Eligibility and the boring but important part

Sports betting is age-restricted and jurisdiction-restricted. 21+ in most US states, 18+ where permitted. Bet only where it is legal for you to do so, and only with money you can afford to lose.

Most bettors lose over the long run. Nothing on this site changes that, and anything that claims to is selling you something.
