The 2026 Yankees Score Four Runs 56.6 Percent Of The Time, 59.0 On The Road, And The Break Even Is 56.52

A team total over 3.5 at -130 asks one question: does this team score four runs more than 56.52 percent of the time? For the 2026 Yankees, the answer depends almost entirely on which sample you pick, and that is what this study measures.

The BetLegend card added Yankees team total over 3.5 at -130 for 3 units tonight at Arizona, against Eduardo Rodriguez. Here is how often New York has actually cleared that bar.

The full season sits on the break even line to the tenth of a point. Every sample that matches tonight's setting, a road game in the middle of a hot September, sits above it. The last ten carries the biggest edge and the least weight: ten games is a thin sample, and two of those ten were two run nights against the Mets.

Rodriguez is the reason the total is 3.5. The counterweight is time. At 5.9 innings a start, roughly a third of the game belongs to an Arizona bullpen with a 3.92 ERA and 1.23 WHIP. Arizona starters as a group have a 4.23 ERA, so Rodriguez is well above the staff he pitches on.

The Yankees have a .739 OPS against left handed pitching and .712 against right handers this season, and they have hit 209 home runs.

What the ticket needs

At -130 the ticket risks 3.9 units to win 3. It needs a true rate of 56.52 percent. The road split and the recent form both clear it by a few points, the full season only matches it, and the home split falls short. That makes this a narrow edge built on context, not a large one.

The same game also carries the Yankees moneyline at -110 on this card. Both tickets are exposed to the same risk, a quiet night for the New York lineup.
