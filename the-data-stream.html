The data stream

Sports Betting Prime

This page previously carried an automated data feed. It no longer does.

Sports Betting Prime does not run a live data feed. The automated feed that occupied this page was removed in July 2026 because the statistics it published were not sourced from any data provider.

What replaced it is slower and smaller but checkable: evergreen guides on how markets are priced, calculators that show their own formulas, and dated analysis that names its source and its as-of date.

The full account of what was removed is in our editorial policy.
