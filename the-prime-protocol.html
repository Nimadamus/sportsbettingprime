The Prime protocol

How this site decides what it is willing to publish.

Every number on Sports Betting Prime is one of two things: computed in your browser from figures you entered, or carried with a named source and the date it was current as of. There is no third category.

We publish no live odds, no projections, no consensus percentages, no daily slates and no sharp-money claims. Nothing reaches the site automatically without a person reviewing it. Corrections are made on the page, dated, and listed rather than silently edited.

The rules in full, including what was removed in July 2026 and why, are on the editorial policy page. Who we are and how we make money is on the about page.
