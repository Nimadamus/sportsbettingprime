Performance telemetry

Sports Betting Prime

We do not publish a performance record, and here is why that matters.

Sports Betting Prime does not track or publish a betting record. We are a research site: we explain how markets work and we build tools. We do not sell picks, so there is no record of ours for you to check.

That is a deliberate boundary rather than an omission. A site that publishes analysis and also grades its own results has an obvious incentive problem, and self-reported records are the least reliable artefact in this industry.

For records that are tracked independently — timestamped before the game and graded automatically against the outcome — use Trust My Record. Note that it is operated by the same people as this site; see our affiliate disclosure.
