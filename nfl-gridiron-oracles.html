NFL

NFL Betting Guide: Key Numbers, Spreads and Totals

The most efficiently priced market in North American sport. Understanding key numbers and how a half point is priced is most of the edge available.

No live data on this page. Sports Betting Prime does not publish live odds, projected statistics, consensus percentages or game predictions. Every figure below is either a definition, a worked example computed from the numbers shown, or carries a named source and the date it was current as of.

What actually moves this market

3 and 7 dominate everything

NFL margins cluster on 3 and 7 more than any other number. Moving a spread from -2.5 to -3.5 crosses the single most common margin in the sport, and the price should reflect that far more than a move from -5.5 to -6.5.

Buy the half point that matters, not the one that doesn't

Paying extra to move off 3 is frequently worth it. Paying the same extra to move off 8 usually is not. The value of a half point depends entirely on which number it crosses.

Totals move on weather and pace, not narrative

Wind is the weather variable that reliably moves a football total. Cold on its own does far less than people assume.

The market is thin on Tuesday and deep on Sunday

Early-week NFL numbers carry lower limits and move further on less money. The closing number is the one with real information in it.

Divisional and short-week spots are known

Every situational angle you have read about is in the model. If a spot is well known, it is priced - which does not make it wrong, only unprofitable at face value.
