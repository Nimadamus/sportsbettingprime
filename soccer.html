Soccer Betting Guide: Three-Way Markets and Asian Handicaps

Soccer

The largest betting market in the world, and the one whose structure is least familiar to bettors who started with American sports.

No live data on this page. Sports Betting Prime does not publish live odds, projected statistics, consensus percentages or game predictions. Every figure below is either a definition, a worked example computed from the numbers shown, or carries a named source and the date it was current as of.

What actually moves this market

Three outcomes, not two

The draw is a real result with a real price. A three-way market carries its margin across three selections, which changes how you read the overround.

Asian handicaps remove the draw

By handicapping the match, an Asian line converts a three-way market into a two-way one. Quarter lines such as -0.25 split your stake across two handicaps.

Low scoring means high variance

A sport where 1-0 is a common scoreline produces results that diverge from underlying performance for long stretches.

Expected goals is useful and overused

xG is a better description of chance quality than the scoreline. It is not a prediction, and it is already in the models the market uses.

Team news arrives late and moves prices hard

Confirmed lineups often land around an hour before kickoff. Rotation in congested fixture periods is the single biggest late mover.
