Recent studies

Original data work. Each page names its sample and its range, and none of them is a pick. The full set lives on the studies hub, grouped by NFL, college football and MLB.

NFL

Road underdogs at 5.5 covered 61.9 percent. At 6.5 they were 44 and 44

Eleven seasons and 2,895 NFL games. Closing 5.5 and closing 6.5 are not the same bet.

NFL

Moving an NFL team total from 18.5 to 19.5 buys 1.7 percent. To 20.5 buys 8.1 more

Eleven seasons and 5,790 NFL team games. Teams finish on 20 seven times as often as on 18.

MLB

Baseball totals land on odd numbers 57.87 percent of the time

The half run on a total is not a rounding choice. It is a bet on whether the game can finish on the whole number.

College football

Outgaining your opponent by under 50 yards is a coin flip

1,340 completed 2025 FBS games. A yardage gap under 50 came with a win only 54.87 percent of the time.

MLB

A team total under 4.5 hits 58 percent of the time, until you check who is pitching

The headline rate does not survive contact with the starter. The study names the sample and the split.

What this site does not do

It does not sell or publish picks, does not claim a betting record, and does not rank handicappers. There is no live odds feed attached to it, so it never shows a current price. Consensus publishing has been paused since July 2026 and the reason is on the method page.
