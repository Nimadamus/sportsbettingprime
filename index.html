Outgaining Your Opponent By Under 50 Yards Is A Coin Flip

1,340 completed 2025 FBS games. The loser outgained the winner in 286 of them, and sorted by the size of the edge, a gap under 50 yards came with a win only 54.87 percent of the time. Home field alone was worth 63.21.

Beating An FCS Team By 42 Is Worth Something, Beating One By 20 Is Not

Three seasons and 132 FBS teams whose opener was a win over a non FBS opponent. The 42 point blowouts went 63.2 percent in their next game. The 14 to 27 point wins went 36.8 percent and lost by 10.7 a game.

A Debut Starter Allows Exactly League Average, And His Team

Across 4,398 starting pitcher outings this season, a man making his first career start allowed 4.49 runs against a league average of 4.48. His club still went 41.98 percent, and the reason is on the other side of the box score.
