Road Underdogs Who Close At 5.5 Cover 61.9 Percent. The 6.5s Are 44 And 44

Sunday's card has four underdogs and four different numbers. Jacksonville +2.5, the Jets +4 at home, Minnesota +5.5, Indianapolis +6.5. Two of those prices need 54.95 percent at -122. That is a lot of points to ask from a base rate, so we counted what those closing lines have actually done.

The result is not "dogs cover." It is that 5.5 and 6.5 are not the same bet. Games that close with the road team getting 5.5 have covered 61.9 percent of the time. Games that close at 6.5 are 44 and 44.

What we measured

Every NFL regular season game from 2015 through 2025, 2,895 games, using closing spreads from the nflverse schedule file. 2015 is the first season with the longer extra point. We then kept four slices, one for each ticket on the card, at the exact closing line:

Read the last two rows first. Both tickets on this card are priced at -122. One of those closing lines has cleared 54.95 percent by seven points. The other has not cleared it at all.

The 5.5 market and the 6.5 market are different games

That gap is not a half point of hook value. It is two different groups of games. When we put every road underdog of 5.5 or 6.5 into one pile, 257 games, and then score both lines against the same scoreboard, the hook shows up the way it should:

Thirteen games decided by exactly 6 points. Those are the only games that lose +5.5 and cash +6.5. That is 5.1 percentage points, and it is why the combined sample moves from 52.1 to 57.2 when you buy the extra point. At -122 you need 54.95. On this combined pile, +5.5 is short and +6.5 clears.

The closing line split tells a different story. The market does not put the same kind of game on 5.5 that it puts on 6.5. The 84 games that actually closed at 5.5 covered 61.9 percent. The 88 that closed at 6.5 covered 50.0. Buying 6.5 because "it is more points" is buying the group that has not beaten the juice.

What that does to Sunday

Minnesota is +5.5 at Chicago. That is the 61.9 percent bucket, and 61.9 against 54.95 is the widest gap on the card. Jacksonville is +2.5 at Denver. Road +2.5 has covered 55.3 percent of 159 games, and -103 only needs 50.74. Indianapolis is +6.5 at Kansas City. That is the 50.0 percent bucket against the same 54.95 the Vikings ticket needs. The extra point is not helping.

The Jets are +4 at home at -150. Home +4 is 27, 19 and 1 in 47 games, 58.7 percent. The price needs 60.0. The sample is the smallest of the four, and the number does not clear. That is the honest starting line on MetLife, before anyone talks about Green Bay losing 39-22 last week.

What this study does not tell you

It does not know Minkah Fitzpatrick is out, or that Denver had 176 yards on Monday night, or that Kansas City went 6-11 last season. It cannot see the 79 percent rain chance at Soldier Field. Base rates set the bar. The matchup has to clear it, and on the 6.5 ticket the bar is already above what that closing line has done.

Method: NFL regular season games from the nflverse schedule file, closing spreads as recorded there, 2015 through 2025, 2,895 games with a result and a spread. Home favorite is a positive spread_line. Road +5.5 means the home team closed at 5.5. Cover at +L means the underdog's margin plus L is greater than 0. One home +4 game landed on a push. Sunday's prices were the TrustMyRecord board numbers locked on the four tickets: Jets +4 -150, Vikings +5.5 -122, Jaguars +2.5 -103, Colts +6.5 -122, read 20 September 2026.
