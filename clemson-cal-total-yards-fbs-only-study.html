Cal Runs For 2.8 A Carry Against FBS Teams While Clemson Climbed From 145 Yards To 401: The Box Score Study Behind Clemson At Cal

Clemson and Cal are both 2 and 1, and Clemson is a 2.5 point favorite in Berkeley. Records that match can hide teams that don't. So before looking at the number, here's what the box scores from every 2026 game say, with the FCS game labeled and set aside.

What the price says

DraftKings has Clemson -2.5 with a total of 52.5, according to the odds feed on ESPN's game page. The moneyline is Clemson -142 and Cal +120. Converting those before the bookmaker's margin comes out:

Two things jump out. Clemson's offense has gone up every week, from 145 yards to 347 to 401, and it has outgained both opponents since LSU. Cal has been outgained by both FBS teams it has played, by 51 yards against UCLA and by 188 at Syracuse.

Cal's run game against FBS defenses

Against UCLA and Syracuse, Cal ran 66 times for 186 yards. That's 2.8 a carry. Against Wagner it was 4.8. Adam Mohammed has carried the load, 28 times for 120 yards against UCLA and 19 for 74 at Syracuse, so this isn't a question of who gets the ball.

That puts a lot on Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele. He's 72 of 108 for 866 yards with 7 touchdowns and 2 interceptions, and 413 of those yards came against Wagner. He's the reason this spread isn't bigger.

How Cal won at Syracuse

Syracuse outgained Cal 437 to 249, converted 7 of 17 third downs to Cal's 3 of 12, and lost 21 to 18. The difference was turnovers: Syracuse gave it away 3 times and Cal didn't give it away once. Turnover margin is real when it happens, but it's the least repeatable number in a box score.

Clemson's pass rush

Will Heldt had 2 sacks against Georgia Southern and 2 more against North Carolina. Georgia Southern ran 18 times for 22 yards. The caveat sits right next to it: North Carolina ran for 214 on 39 carries last week, and LSU ran for 309 in week one. Clemson's run defense isn't settled.

What this adds up to

Clemson has the better yardage profile in every FBS game since week one, and Cal's two FBS results both came with Cal gaining fewer yards than it allowed. The case against Clemson is the LSU game, the run defense, and a 10:30 PM Eastern kickoff three time zones from home. BetLegend's play on this game is Clemson -2.5 at -110 for 3 units, logged before kickoff on TrustMyRecord.
