Outgaining Your Opponent By Under 50 Yards Is A Coin Flip

1,340 completed 2025 FBS games. The loser outgained the winner in 286 of them, and sorted by the size of the edge, a gap under 50 yards came with a win only 54.87 percent of the time. Home field alone was worth 63.21.

A Five Win Hot Streak Edge Won 53.6 Percent, Less Than A One Win Edge

2,134 games of the 2026 MLB season. A bigger last ten edge never bought a higher win rate; the season record did, and cold teams scored a little less.

A .100 To .199 Record Edge Won 50.6 Percent Without An Ace And 68.5 With One

1,963 games of the 2026 MLB season sorted by pregame record gap and starter ERA. The standings edge barely moves the win rate; a starter under 3.00 moves it nearly 20 points.

All 4,512 team games of the 2026 MLB season sorted by starter ERA tier. Only the top tier clears the price on a team total under 3.5, and a -1 run line has to be judged on the games that do not push.

NFL Road Underdogs Of 1.5 To 2.5 Lose By Exactly 3 More Often Than By 1 And 2

Eleven seasons and 246 short road underdogs. They lost by exactly 3 more often than by 1 and 2 combined, and road teams implied near 20 points finished on exactly 20 almost three times as often as on 21.

Moving An NFL Team Total From 18.5 To 19.5 Buys 1.7 Percent, To 20.5 Buys 8.1 More

Eleven seasons and 5,790 NFL team games. Teams finish on 20 seven times as often as on 18, so the hook on a team total is nearly free in the dead zone and expensive next to 17, 20, 24 and 27.

Beating An FCS Team By 42 Is Worth Something, Beating One By 20 Is Not

Three seasons and 132 FBS teams whose opener was a win over a non FBS opponent. The 42 point blowouts went 63.2 percent in their next game. The 14 to 27 point wins went 36.8 percent and lost by 10.7 a game.
