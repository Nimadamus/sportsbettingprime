What you should know before you sign up. JustBet is an offshore operator. It is not licensed by a US state regulator, which means the consumer protections that come with a regulated book — state dispute resolution, mandated segregated funds, statewide self-exclusion — do not apply. Bonus terms carry rollover requirements that are easy to underestimate. If a regulated book is available where you live, that is the safer option, and we would rather tell you that than lose the point.

The advice that is actually worth more than any bonus

Line shopping. Holding accounts at more than one book and taking the best available price on each bet is the largest reliable edge available to a recreational bettor, and it is larger than any signup offer. A half point on an NFL spread, or four cents of price on a moneyline, compounds across a season in a way a one-off bonus does not.

If you take one thing from this page, take that one, and read why odds differ between sportsbooks to understand where the differences come from.

How we choose what to list

We list books we would use ourselves and we say what is wrong with them. We do not list a book purely because it pays well, and we have declined placements. When an operator's terms change we update the page or remove it; the operator's own terms always take precedence over anything written here.
